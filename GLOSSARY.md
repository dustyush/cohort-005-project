# Course Platform

Learners buy courses and watch their lessons. This glossary fixes the words we use for buying a course and for asking for the money back.

## Language

### Buying

**Learner**:
A person who buys courses and watches their lessons.
_Avoid_: Customer, student, buyer

**Purchase**:
One Learner buying one course.
_Avoid_: Order, transaction

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's request to get back the money paid for a Purchase. Our team approves or declines each one by hand.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, cancel my purchase, reversal, chargeback

**Refund**:
The money given back to a learner when a Refund Request is approved. An approved Refund ends the Purchase and takes away the learner's access to the course.
_Avoid_: Reversal, chargeback

**Refund Window**:
The period in which a learner can make a Refund Request, from the day of the Purchase up to and including the 14th calendar day after it.
_Avoid_: 14-day window, cancellation period

**Refundable Amount**:
The amount a Refund would give back, which is always what the learner actually paid for the Purchase.
_Avoid_: Refund price, course price

**Requested** / **Approved** / **Declined**:
The three states of a Refund Request. Every request starts as Requested and is then either Approved or Declined.
_Avoid_: Pending, open
